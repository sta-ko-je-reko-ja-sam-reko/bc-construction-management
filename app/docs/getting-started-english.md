# Construction Management

Construction Management turns Business Central into a system for running construction projects — from the first estimate through scheduling, billing, subcontractors and plant, all on top of your standard projects and finances.

It ships as separate modules, so you enable and pay for only the ones you need. Whatever you turn on appears on the **Construction Manager** home page and works together automatically.

## What the app covers

- **Estimating** — build a Bill of Quantities and turn it into the project's budget.
- **Cost control** — see costs broken down across the project, track committed cost, and forecast the final cost.
- **Scheduling** — plan the work as a task outline with dates, dependencies and crews, and view it on a Gantt chart.
- **Progress billing** — bill the customer in stages against a schedule of values and issue payment certificates.
- **Retention** — automatically withhold and later release retention on customer invoices and subcontractor claims.
- **Subcontracts** — manage subcontract agreements, progress claims and variations.
- **Equipment & plant** — register machines and tools, set rates, and record usage against projects.
- **AI assistants** — connect Copilot so it can read and update your construction data.

## Good to know

- **Enable only what you need.** Features you haven't turned on stay hidden and don't get in the way — turn them on any time from **Set up Construction Management**.
- **The session restarts once** after you enable or disable features, so the changes take effect. This is normal.
- **It builds on your existing projects and finances.** Construction Management extends standard projects, customers and vendors rather than replacing them, so your usual posting, invoicing and reporting still apply.
