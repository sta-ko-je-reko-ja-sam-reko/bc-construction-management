# FEAT-CST-001 - Cost Control & Forecasting

Track committed cost and forecast final cost per project. Requires the **Cost Control** module.

## Steps

1. Open a construction **Project** and choose **Cost Control**.
2. Review per task: **Budget / Committed / Actual / ETC / EAC / Variance / % Complete**.
3. **Committed** reflects open purchase orders linked to the project; **EAC** is the forecast final cost.
4. Adjust **% Complete** on a task to refine the forecast, or accept the cost-based default.
