# FEAT-RC-001 - Construction Manager Role Center

The **Construction Manager** Role Center is the home page for running construction projects — activity tiles and quick navigation for every feature you've enabled.

## Switch to the role

1. Choose the **Settings** (gear) icon → **My Settings**.
2. In **Role**, choose the assist-edit and pick **Construction Manager**.
3. Choose **OK**. Your home page becomes the Construction Manager Role Center.

(Administrators can assign this role to users in **User Personalizations** / via a permission/role assignment.)

## What you'll see

- **Activity tiles**, grouped per feature — Projects (construction & active projects), Estimating (bills of quantities), Progress Billing (applications), Subcontracts (subcontracts, change orders, claims), Equipment & Plant (equipment, in maintenance), Scheduling (scheduled tasks). Only the groups for features you have enabled appear. Choose a tile to open its list.
- The **project Gantt** for a current scheduled project, right on the home page.
- **Navigation** down the left and **+ New** / **Process** actions across the top — again, only for the features you have.

> The tiles' numbers are calculated in the background, so the page opens immediately and the counts appear a moment later. If a feature's tiles or actions are missing, enable the feature from **Set up Construction Management** (see the assisted-setup getting-started).
